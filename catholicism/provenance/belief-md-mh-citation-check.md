# Check of the *Magnifica Humanitas* citations in `catholicism/BELIEF.md`

**Date:** 19 November 2026
**What was checked:** every place `BELIEF.md` cites *Magnifica Humanitas* (MH) by paragraph number (34 citation instances on 24 lines, counting each repeated cite in a line). Each was compared with the text of the cited paragraph, using the Word copy of the encyclical supplied by the repo owner. The owner reports that the Word copy was compared with the live Vatican pages (not independently re-checked here).
**What was not checked:** every citation to other documents in the same sentences (*Dignitas Infinita*, *Gaudium et Spes*, *Laudato Si'*, *Laborem Exercens*, *Tertio Millennio Adveniente*, and so on). Those need their own texts. Nothing here is a theological review; it says only whether the cited paragraph contains what the sentence attributes to it.

**Result:** every cited paragraph exists and is on the topic; none is a wrong paragraph number. In 10 of the cases below (second table) the sentence says more than the paragraph does, so the cite supports only part of it. The rest hold as written (first table). **Update, 19 November 2026:** the fixes below were applied to `BELIEF.md`, except two rows that turned out not to need one. In the MH 48-53 row, ¶50 does say people are "created in the image and likeness ... of the triune god", so that cite holds as written. In the MH 46-81 row, a keyword search found each principle inside the range (universal destination ¶64-67, subsidiarity ¶67-73, solidarity ¶72-76, option for the poor ¶78); the range was left as is, and the paragraphs were not read one by one. Also, ¶50 states that dignity does not depend on "the right or wrong choices made", so the line 52 fix now cites ¶50 as well as ¶52. Line 144 still relies on *Dignitas Infinita* part IV for "trafficked, imprisoned, dying" and "does not come in grades"; that document has not been checked.
